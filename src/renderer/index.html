Which 1Password account should your documents go to?

  1. Open the 1Password app.
  2. Find your account's name at the very top of the sidebar, above your vaults.
  3. Type it below exactly as shown.

Otherwise one approval covers everything you do while the window is open. You can change this later from the menu-bar icon.

PaperRoute checks the name with 1Password straight away, so 1Password may ask you to approve. You only do this once.
